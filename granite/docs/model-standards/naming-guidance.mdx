title: Naming Guidance
description: Guidance for naming derivatives of Granite models.
"icon": "tag"
---

If you release a derivative of Granite, please label derivatives with your entity name at the beginning, followed by “Granite,” and then adding any further model descriptors for your modified model at the end: e.g. `acme-Granite-reasoning`.

If possible, attaching the notices file to the Granite model or its download site would be appreciated: `NOTICE.txt`.

To help others understand your model’s lineage

If you create and distribute a derivative of a Granite model, please label derivatives with your entity name at the beginning, followed by “Granite,” and then adding any further model descriptors for your modified model at the end: e.g. `acme-Granite-reasoning`.  
Include the full model base name (e.g., `Granite-3.3-8B-Instruct`) in documentation or metadata.  
Avoid using "Granite" alone in your project title to prevent confusion with official IBM releases.
