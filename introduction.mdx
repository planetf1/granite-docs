## What is IBM Granite?

IBM Granite models are large language models (LLMs) and multimodal models trained on enterprise-relevant data with a focus on:

- **Enterprise-ready**: Built for business applications with governance and compliance in mind
- **Transparent**: Open sourcing code, data governance, and model development processes  
- **Performant**: Competitive performance across language understanding, generation, and reasoning tasks
- **Responsible**: Developed with AI ethics and responsible AI principles
